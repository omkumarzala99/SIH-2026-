# Platform Demonstration & Operational Walkthrough

A structured demonstration walkthrough showcasing the complete mining intelligence decision loop.

---

## Demonstration Storyboard

### Phase 1: The Mining Reality & Executive Dashboard
1. Open the web application at `http://localhost:5173`.
2. Present the **Executive Dashboard**:
   - Review the 4 primary KPI cards: Reserves (1,485,000 t), Scheduled Target (1,000 t/day), Projected Shortfall, and Composite Risk Score.
   - Interact with the **Spatial Concession GIS Map**: Toggle the Concession Boundary, Operational Pits, and AI Reserve Heatmap layers.
   - Review the 14-day production trend chart illustrating how rainfall and blasting delays create extraction deficits.

### Phase 2: The Crisis Scenario (Discover &rarr; Predict &rarr; Detect)
1. Click the prominent **"Run Crisis Simulation"** button in the navigation bar.
2. An alert banner confirms the crisis conditions:
   - Heavy Monsoon cloudburst (64.2mm)
   - Saturated pit floor (62% soil moisture)
   - Hydraulic failure on CAT-349 Excavator (6.5 hours downtime)
   - Wet blast holes causing 2.5 hours detonation delay.
3. Switch to **Risk Monitoring**:
   - Observe the risk score escalating to **HIGH / CRITICAL (74.5/100)**.
   - Review the **Explainable AI (XAI)** cards: Note that the system mathematically attributes the root causes to equipment downtime (35% weight) and rainfall impact (25% weight).

### Phase 3: Decision Support & What-If Simulation
1. Switch to **Decision Support & Recommendations**:
   - Review the prescriptive actions generated by the AI:
     - Re-deploy haul dumpers to Pit A upper bench (+110 tons recovery)
     - Reschedule bench blasting window to Shift-B (+95 tons recovery)
     - Activate auxiliary submersible sump pumps (+75 tons recovery).
   - Execute **Human-in-the-Loop Governance**: Click **Approve** on the priority action and enter an official Mine Manager audit note.
2. Switch to **What-If Simulation**:
   - Adjust the equipment downtime slider from 6.5h down to 2.0h and rainfall down to 20mm.
   - Click **"Execute What-If Simulation"**: Examine the side-by-side **BEFORE vs. AFTER** comparison displaying recovered tonnages and risk reduction.
3. Review the **Data Quality & Governance** tab: Inspect non-fabricated empirical quality scores (completeness, uniqueness, freshness) across all 5 datasets.
