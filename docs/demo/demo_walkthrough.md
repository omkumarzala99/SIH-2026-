# Hackathon Presentation Walkthrough: PS-26009

A 3-minute demonstration script for hackathon judges showcasing the complete intelligence loop.

---

## 3-Minute Presentation Storyboard

### Minute 1: The Mining Reality & Executive Dashboard
1. Open the web application at `http://localhost:5173`.
2. Present the **Executive Dashboard**:
   - Show the 4 KPI cards: Reserves (1,485,000 t), Scheduled Target (1,000 t/day), Projected Shortfall, and Composite Risk Score.
   - Point to the **Spatial Concession GIS Map**: Toggle the Concession Boundary, Operational Pits, and AI Reserve Heatmap layers.
   - Show the 14-day production trend chart illustrating how rainfall and blasting delays create extraction gaps.

### Minute 2: The Crisis Scenario (Discover &rarr; Predict &rarr; Detect)
1. Click the prominent **"Run SIH Crisis Demo"** button in the navigation bar.
2. An alert banner confirms the crisis conditions:
   - Heavy Monsoon cloudburst (64.2mm)
   - Saturated pit floor (62% soil moisture)
   - Hydraulic failure on CAT-349 Excavator (6.5 hours downtime)
   - Wet blast holes causing 2.5 hours detonation delay.
3. Switch to **Risk Monitoring**:
   - Show the risk score escalating to **HIGH / CRITICAL (74.5/100)**.
   - Highlight the **Explainable AI (XAI)** cards: Show the judges that the system clearly attributes the root causes to equipment downtime (35% weight) and rainfall impact (25% weight).

### Minute 3: Decision Support & What-If Simulation
1. Switch to **Decision Support & Recommendations**:
   - Demonstrate the prescriptive actions generated by the AI:
     - Re-deploy haul dumpers to Pit A upper bench (+110 tons recovery)
     - Reschedule bench blasting window to Shift-B (+95 tons recovery)
     - Activate auxiliary submersible sump pumps (+75 tons recovery).
   - Demonstrate **Human-in-the-Loop**: Click **Approve** on the priority action and enter a brief Mine Manager audit note.
2. Switch to **What-If Simulation**:
   - Move the equipment downtime slider from 6.5h down to 2.0h and rainfall down to 20mm.
   - Click **"Execute What-If Simulation"**: Show the side-by-side **BEFORE vs. AFTER** comparison displaying recovered tonnages and risk reduction.
3. Show the **Data Quality & Governance** tab: Demonstrate non-fabricated empirical quality scores (completeness, uniqueness, freshness) across all 5 datasets.
