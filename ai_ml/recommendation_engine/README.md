# Decision Support & Recommendation Engine (Member 3 Ownership)

Prescriptive intelligence engine that translates production shortfall predictions and multi-factor operational risks into prioritized, actionable interventions for Mine Managers.

## Core Operational Actions
- **Equipment Re-Deployment**: Re-allocates dumpers and shovels from bottlenecked pits to active high-grade faces.
- **Blasting Schedule Optimization**: Re-sequences bench shot-firing windows around rainfall spikes to avoid wet misfires.
- **Zone Prioritization**: Guides extraction toward high-reserve benches to maintain required ore blending grades.
- **Dewatering & Ramp Maintenance**: Dispatches auxiliary sump pumps and grading crews to restore transit speed.

## Human-in-the-Loop Protocol
AI predictions never automate irreversible physical operations.
1. **Engine Proposes**: Suggests recommended action, expected impact, and tonnage recovery.
2. **Mine Manager Reviews**: Inspects root cause and risk factor attribution.
3. **Action Executed**: Manager clicks **Approve**, **Reject**, or **Modify** with written notes recorded in the audit trail.
